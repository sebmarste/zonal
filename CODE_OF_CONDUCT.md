# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss ideas and behavior, give
constructive feedback, and make room for different experience levels.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexual harassment, and publishing
someone else's private information are not acceptable. Do not use project spaces
for spam or repeated personal attacks.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed spaces.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, close disruptive conversations, or restrict
participation. Responses should reflect the severity and repetition of the behavior.

For a private report or an appeal, email **zonal@sebmarste.de**. Include the relevant
links and context; avoid sharing unrelated personal information. Reports will be
handled as privately as practical, with information shared only as needed to
investigate and respond.
